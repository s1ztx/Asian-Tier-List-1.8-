Understand how the Asian Tier List works

🏅 Tier System

The Asian Tier List uses a 14-tier system from S (highest) to D- (lowest). Players are ranked based on their performance across seven gamemodes.

⚖️ Ranking Rules

  • 1. Testing Process: All players must be tested by an official tester to receive a rank.
  • 2. Fair Play: Any form of cheating, hacking, or exploiting will result in immediate removal from the rankings.
  • 3. Respect: All players must treat testers and staff with respect. Toxic behavior will not be tolerated.
  • 4. Rank Updates: Ranks can be updated based on performance and activity. Players may request re-evaluation.
  • 5. One Account: Players can only have one active ranking. Duplicate accounts will be merged or removed.

🎯 Tester Guidelines

  • 1. Objectivity: Testers must remain objective and fair when evaluating players.
  • 2. Documentation: All tests must be properly documented with date, gamemode, and result.
  • 3. Communication: Testers should provide constructive feedback to players after testing.
  • 4. Confidentiality: Test results are private until officially published.
  • 5. Activity: Testers must remain active and responsive to test requests.

🤝 Community Guidelines

  • 1. Discord Conduct: Follow Discord server rules at all times.
  • 2. Reviews: Reviews must be honest and constructive. Fake or toxic reviews will be removed.
  • 3. Support: Use the support system for any issues or disputes.
  • 4. Applications: Staff and tester applications must be honest and complete.
  • 5. Privacy: Do not share personal information of other players without consent.
